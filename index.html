BACKUP & SETTINGS · Protect your progress

Your study records live in this browser, not your GitHub account. This section is for preserving or restoring that data.

How: Tap “Export backup” to save your notes and progress. Use “Import backup” only to restore a previously exported backup. Do not select “Erase local data” unless you want to delete your progress.

Privacy and portability

Your data, your device.

Back up your study

Reading history, devotional progress, passages, plan progress, and notes stay in this browser's local storage. Export a JSON backup regularly so that clearing browser data or changing devices does not erase your work.

Import replaces local study data after confirmation.

Offline use

Opening the downloaded index.html file works entirely offline. To install as a home-screen web app, host the included folder on HTTPS or localhost once, then use your browser's Add to Home Screen / Install option.

The app does not sync between devices. The first-version reader tracks all 1,189 chapters, but does not contain the full Bible text.
